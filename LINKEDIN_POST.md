Safe to Send · Ep 18 — You can't automate what you haven't standardised

Four markets, four "welcome journeys". I scored each against one written definition: required steps in order, plus two guards every version needs (exit on unsubscribe, consent check).

Croatia: 100%
Austria: 86%
Greece: 71%
Hungary: 14%

The surprise was Austria. It looked the most thorough, with extra waits and a preference step, and it was the only one that keeps emailing people after they unsubscribe.

Hungary isn't a welcome journey at all. It's a monthly batch to everyone new, wearing a welcome journey's name.

This is the problem with "let's add an AI agent to check our journeys": the agent needs a shared definition of what right looks like. Most teams have ten markets' worth of opinions instead.

The repo has 10 journey patterns as plain data. Steal them, change them, and agree on them before you automate anything.
